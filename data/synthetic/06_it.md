## Request channel

Every IT request is recorded in the IT board with a title, affected environment, impact and attempted steps. Screenshots must remove secrets and personal data. Instant messaging is used to flag an urgent ticket, not to replace the record. An issue affecting several people is grouped under one incident. The requester receives a tracking reference and next contact point. This prevents contradictory duplicate requests and makes it possible to distinguish an individual setup problem from a shared service failure.

## Priority

A completely unusable workstation blocking critical work receives high priority. Degradation with a workable alternative receives normal priority. Equipment and software requests are scheduled work. Priority can change after diagnosis, with the reason recorded. Internal support objectives are distinct from customer SLA terms. A suspected compromise immediately follows the security incident process rather than ordinary troubleshooting. The requester should report observed impact instead of guessing a severity level to obtain faster attention.

## Access and devices

An access request states the resource, duration, required role and resource-owner approval. Platform grants the minimum necessary rights and checks the result. Accounts are individual and use MFA where available. Temporary access has an expiry date. Quarterly reviews compare groups with current responsibilities. Service accounts have an owner and are not used for personal login. Devices are encrypted and automatically lock after five minutes. Browser extensions that can read customer pages need review before installation.

## Resolution

Approved professional spaces are backed up; a local desktop folder is not a reliable archive. Lost equipment is reported immediately, and security considers active-session revocation. The technician records known cause, action and a reproducible verification. A ticket closes after confirmation or two reminders two working days apart, while remaining reopenable. A recurring solution becomes a sanitized troubleshooting note. No credentials belong in tickets, including closed records and exported reports. Account removal follows departure controls and preserves authorized evidence.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. ## Worked example

Two people report the same service error. Support links their requests to one incident, records the shared impact and provides one next-update time. Individual troubleshooting notes remain useful, but duplicate investigation does not create competing recovery decisions.
