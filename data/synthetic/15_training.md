# Training and knowledge sharing

It is not actual IAWEB.DEV material and does not represent its policies, employees or customers. All names, rules, durations and values belong only to this demonstration scenario. ## Allowance

The fictional annual learning allowance is 600 euros per person with two planned training days. It can fund courses, workshops or resources tied to an approved professional objective. Available budget does not automatically authorize spending. The manager checks relevance and capacity; Operations confirms booking arrangements. A payment made before recorded approval has no automatic reimbursement promise. These values are portfolio assumptions and do not establish real employment benefits or a legal entitlement for any person.

## Request process

A request identifies the skill, proposed resource, price, dates and an application expected within three months. It mentions free or internal alternatives considered. A broad goal such as improving API design becomes a concrete outcome, for example proposing an error convention and testing it on one route. The manager responds within five working days and explains a postponement by capacity or priority. The person can revise the proposal rather than interpreting a scheduling delay as rejection of professional development.

## Scheduling and sharing

Training days are visible in the capacity calendar and active work has a backup. They are not personal leave. Sessions outside shared hours need an agreed arrangement rather than an implied expectation of extra work. Paid bookings follow purchasing rules; related travel follows the travel guide. Within ten working days after training, the participant writes a short note or gives a twenty-minute session. Copyrighted materials are not redistributed without permission. The share identifies useful ideas, limits and a small experiment.

## Application and community

After three months the manager and participant review practical effects and obstacles. A small measured experiment is preferred to an unevaluated organization-wide change. Internal knowledge sessions take place every other Friday after the demonstration. They may cover sanitized incidents, engineering techniques or architecture decisions. Newcomers may initially observe. Resulting notes receive titles, owners and review dates. A skills directory supports cooperation and must not publish individual HR assessments or automatic judgments generated by an AI tool.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

## Worked example

A participant attends an API course and proposes a twenty-minute internal session. The share includes one usable error-handling experiment and its limitations. Three months later, the manager checks whether that experiment actually improved delivery rather than merely counting attendance.
