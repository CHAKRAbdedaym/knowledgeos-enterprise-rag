## Planned leave

Planned leave is requested ten working days before the intended start. An absence longer than five working days should ideally be requested twenty working days ahead. The request includes dates and an operational handover, without intimate personal justification. The manager responds within three working days after checking coverage. Leave is confirmed only when approval is recorded. These timelines are fictional planning rules, not legal entitlements. Buying travel does not convert a pending request into an approved absence.

## Handover

Before leaving, the employee records active files, upcoming deadlines, risks and the named backup. On-call duties, if any, are explicitly reassigned. An out-of-office message states return date and a work contact. Passwords are never handed to the backup; required rights follow the IT access process. Deliverables due during absence are rescheduled or assigned to another owner. A project folder should allow the backup to understand the next action without contacting the person who is away.

## Unexpected absence

An unexpected absence is reported to the manager and Operations as soon as reasonably possible. Medical information is not placed in project channels. The manager organizes continuity and limits requests to the absent person. Any required documents go through restricted people administration. If a delivery cannot be maintained, Delivery tells the business owner the impact and proposes a decision. The board must reflect the new situation instead of quietly keeping an impossible date or expecting the absent person to compensate remotely.

## Remote work and exceptions

Up to three remote-work days per week are allowed with manager approval. Shared hours remain 10:00–16:00 Paris time. The calendar states the working location for in-person planning. A managed device, locked screen and VPN for private resources are required. Working from another country needs prior approval from Operations and the manager; tax, contractual and security questions require a separate review. Remote work does not replace leave. A prolonged connectivity problem is reported and an alternative working location is agreed.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. ## Worked example

An employee requests seven working days off three weeks before departure. The manager checks coverage and discusses the preferred twenty-working-day notice, then records a decision. The employee prepares a backup without copying passwords or confidential personal information.
