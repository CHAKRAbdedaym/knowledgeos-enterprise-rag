## Prior approval

Travel needs manager approval and Operations confirmation before paid booking. The request states purpose, location, dates, participants and estimated budget. Consider whether a remote meeting meets the objective. Accepting a customer invitation does not authorize spending. Travel appears in the capacity calendar and active work has a backup. International trips require specific review; this guide supplies no country-specific visa or insurance rule. The traveler should know the approved assistance contact before departure instead of inventing one during a problem.

## Transport

Second-class rail is preferred when a journey can be completed by train in less than four hours. Exceptions explain timing, accessibility or total cost. Compare changeable fares against cancellation risk. Rental cars need specific approval. Personal vehicle use cannot be assumed from convenience alone. Operations specifies the booking channel so approvals and receipts remain traceable. An apparently cheaper booking may still create unacceptable change or safety constraints, so the decision records the factors that materially affected the choice.

## Fictional limits

The indicative hotel ceiling is 120 euros per night excluding breakfast. The meal ceiling is 25 euros per meal. An anticipated excess needs approval before booking; an unexpected constraint is reported with justification. These are portfolio assumptions, not actual benefits. Real taxes, local rules and applicable terms must be checked separately. This corpus defines no mileage allowance or flat per diem. A reader must not derive an unlisted entitlement by treating an illustrative ceiling as a universal reimbursement rule.

## Receipts and safety

Submit the expense report within ten working days after return. Each item has date, professional purpose, currency and a legible receipt; personal purchases are separate. Operations targets processing within fifteen working days after a complete file is received. This is a fictional administration target, not a bank promise. Use the managed device, shield the screen and avoid confidential conversations in public. Private resources require VPN. Lost equipment follows incident reporting immediately. Meeting decisions go into the project folder with owners and deadlines.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

## Worked example

A hotel option exceeds the illustrative ceiling. The traveler requests an exception before paying and records the reason and approval. Operations later matches the receipt to that decision instead of treating the higher amount as automatically reimbursable.
