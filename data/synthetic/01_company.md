## Mission and boundaries

IAWEB.DEV here denotes a fictional digital agency with 24 people. Its demonstration headquarters is in Lyon, France. The agency builds B2B web platforms, API integrations and document assistants for fictional European small and medium-sized businesses. It does not offer health-data hosting or legal advice. The working principle is: an explainable decision and a verifiable deliverable. This organization is an assumption created for a portfolio; it describes no actual company, employee or customer.

## Decision ownership

The three teams are Delivery, Platform and Operations. Delivery builds customer projects and leads demonstrations. Platform maintains shared tools and environments. Operations manages contracts, purchasing, people administration and invoicing coordination. The Delivery lead checks capacity before a commercial commitment. The security contact reviews technical exceptions. Operations maintains the decision register. These are fictional functional roles without real personal identities. A role title in this corpus does not imply that a real individual holds that position.

## Working rhythm

The reference working week is 35 hours, Monday through Friday. Shared collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 Paris time, with a flexible lunch break. Monday capacity planning lasts 25 minutes. Thursday risk review lasts 20 minutes. Friday demonstrations show completed behavior, remaining limitations and the next decisions. Meeting invitations name the time zone. A person working in another time zone must arrange availability with the manager rather than silently interpreting Paris hours as local hours.

## Communication and escalation

Project decisions belong in the project folder with a date, rationale and follow-up owner. An instant message is not a substitute for an approved decision. Availability emergencies follow the incident and SLA procedures. People administration goes to Operations; a security concern follows the incident process even while its impact is uncertain. Sensitive messages stay in restricted channels. The service catalog defines offers, the project lifecycle defines acceptance gates, and security defines technical requirements. An approved customer contract takes precedence over an informal internal note.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

## Worked example

A Friday demonstration reveals a capacity conflict. Delivery records the affected commitment, Platform confirms the technical dependency, and Operations checks the administrative constraint. The final decision states the chosen scope and who will inform the business owner.
