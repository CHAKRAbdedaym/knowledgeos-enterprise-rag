# People practices and collaboration

It is not actual IAWEB.DEV material and does not represent its policies, employees or customers. All names, rules, durations and values belong only to this demonstration scenario. Do not use this text as professional or legal advice.

## Scope and privacy

This guide describes collaboration in a fictional 24-person agency. It is not a contract, legal handbook or statement of actual employment benefits. Operations handles administrative questions. The manager handles daily priorities and professional growth. Personal information is shared only with authorized recipients. The examples do not reveal a real employee’s pay, status or benefits. A person may ask Operations directly when a concern involves their own manager or requires a more private channel.

## Goals and feedback

Quarterly goals state an observable result, deadline and available resources. A 30-minute individual conversation occurs every two weeks. It covers workload, obstacles, progress and cooperation. Notes retain useful decisions rather than unnecessary personal detail. The employee can correct factual errors. Commit counts and individual ticket totals are not performance rankings. A goal that depends on access, equipment or another team must include those dependencies so that missing support is not misread as personal underperformance.

## Capacity and availability

The scenario uses a 35-hour reference week and common hours of 10:00–16:00 Paris time. An expected overload is raised before committing to it; the response is a scope or capacity decision. Being online outside working hours does not create an obligation to reply immediately. Any on-call arrangement is separately planned. A company phone does not imply a permanent support obligation. A blocked task should have an identified next action rather than generating invisible extra work.

## Growth and concerns

A workplace concern can be raised with the manager or Operations. It is received in a restricted space with an explanation of next steps. Facts are separated from interpretations and individuals are not exposed in the public wiki. This fictional guide does not define an exhaustive legal process. Each person proposes a quarterly learning objective and a knowledge-sharing contribution. The fictional learning allowance is 600 euros per person per year with two planned training days; paid booking requires manager approval beforehand.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

## Worked example

A quarterly goal slips because a dependency remains unavailable. The manager records the blocked input, adjusts the plan and identifies support. The discussion does not use raw commit count as a substitute for understanding the actual outcome.
