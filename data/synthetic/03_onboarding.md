# Employee onboarding

It is not actual IAWEB.DEV material and does not represent its policies, employees or customers. All names, rules, durations and values belong only to this demonstration scenario. Do not use this text as professional or legal advice.

Fictional version: 1.0 · Reviewed: 2026-10-01 · Next review: 2027-01-01

## Before arrival

Operations confirms the start date, role, manager and equipment five working days before arrival. Platform prepares a managed laptop, individual work account and only the necessary groups. Passwords are never emailed. The manager appoints an onboarding buddy and prepares a first objective achievable within one week. Administrative records use the restricted people channel and never enter the general knowledge base. The buddy helps with orientation but does not approve their own requests for privileged customer access.

## First day

The first day includes checking the device and activating multi-factor authentication. The newcomer reads security rules and learns the incident-reporting route. The buddy introduces the teams and the shared 10:00–16:00 Paris collaboration window. The manager explains expectations and the first deliverable. An access problem becomes an IT request with an owner. Borrowing a colleague’s account is forbidden even if it appears to be the fastest way to finish an exercise.

## First week

The newcomer installs the local environment, reads engineering conventions and submits a small reviewed contribution. Customer environment access requires a separate request approved by the resource owner. Operations explains leave and remote work. A 45-minute session covers classification and permitted AI use. The individual onboarding checklist is accessible only to the people supporting the newcomer. Completion means demonstrating that a task works, not simply confirming that several tools have been downloaded.

## Follow-up and departure

At day 7 the manager and buddy check whether the newcomer can start the project, run tests and find a recorded decision. Day 30 reviews results, obstacles and training. Day 60 clarifies ongoing responsibilities. These are fictional management milestones, not a legal probation policy. A transfer triggers an access-group review within two working days. On departure, Operations coordinates equipment return and Platform ends sessions at the agreed time, transfers document ownership and rotates any affected secrets.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

## Worked example

On day 7, the newcomer runs the local project but cannot access a customer environment. The buddy records successful local setup while the separate access request stays open. Missing authorization is never bypassed by borrowing credentials.
