## Where to ask for help

For missing access or a broken workstation, create an IT board request with context, impact and a sanitized screenshot. For administration or absence, contact Operations through its restricted channel. For project priority, contact Delivery and the business owner. For suspected leakage, use the incident process immediately. A vague request can be redirected, but sensitive details do not belong in a general space. An instant message should refer to a ticket or decision rather than creating a parallel untracked process.

## Can priorities change

The business owner arbitrates priority with the team. New urgent work states value, effort, risk and effect on the committed outcome. Record the change before promising a date. A developer may propose a smaller solution or workaround but does not independently commit to extra delivery. Iterations last two weeks and risks are reviewed on Thursday. Blocked items keep an owner and next action. A deadline remains meaningful only if the capacity and dependency assumptions supporting it are also visible.

## Can I work remotely

The demonstration allows up to three remote-work days weekly with manager approval. Shared hours are 10:00–16:00 Paris time. Working from another country requires prior review by Operations and the manager. Planned leave is requested ten working days ahead; an absence longer than five working days is ideally prepared twenty working days ahead. Approval must be recorded. Remote work still requires a managed device, screen locking and the VPN for private resources. It is not a substitute for taking necessary leave.

## AI and missing information

Public and synthetic data can be used for experiments in approved tools. Confidential and Restricted data need explicit review and authorization before external AI processing. Results are checked against sources. Missing documentary evidence is not filled with invention. This corpus contains no individual pay, actual revenue, real customer contracts or legal decisions applicable to a person. It provides no cooking recipes, sports results or astronomical ephemerides. Conflicting notes require a document-owner decision and an updated reference before publication.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

A colleague asks for an actual executive salary. The FAQ explains that no such personal record exists in this synthetic corpus. The assistant does not infer a number from company size, location, job titles or illustrative travel and learning allowances.
