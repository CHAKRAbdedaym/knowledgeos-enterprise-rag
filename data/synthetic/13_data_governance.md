## Classes and ownership

Data classes are Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Public content can be distributed after editorial approval. Internal stays in authorized professional spaces. Confidential includes nonpublic customer information. Restricted covers secrets and tightly limited records. Each dataset has an owner who decides use, retention and access groups. Classification does not replace analysis of purpose or applicable rights. A transformed dataset or vector index remains derived information with governance obligations rather than becoming automatically safe because its representation has changed.

## Minimal collection

A new processing activity records its purpose, necessary fields, source and recipients. Unnecessary data is not collected for convenience. Test environments use synthetic records. Production copies need a separate controlled transformation procedure. Logs exclude passwords, tokens and personal-data bodies. Exports are dated and assigned to an owner to prevent abandoned shared-drive copies. A request to collect more fields should explain the decision each field supports and how access and deletion will be managed before ingestion begins.

## Fictional retention periods

Ordinary application logs are retained for 30 days. Security logs are retained for 180 days with restricted access. Project documents are retained for 24 months after closure and then reviewed by the owner. Operational backups use a rolling 30-day window. These are demonstration choices, not universal legal requirements. A real contractual or regulatory duty needs analysis before application and may change the schedule. The absence of a personal name in a file does not by itself establish that the file is non-sensitive.

## Deletion and quality

Deletion covers active sources, search indexes, known exports and authorized working copies. Backups expire on their cycle; arbitrary editing can damage recoverability. A restore reapplies deletions recorded since the backup. External sharing needs owner approval and an expiring link where supported. Documents have a title, owner, review date, category and status. Obsolete notes are marked and archived with a replacement link. The public portfolio knowledge base contains only synthetic material and claims no compliance certification or actual processing of customer personal data.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

## Worked example

A project owner asks to remove a document. The responsible team checks the active file, derived index and known exports, records the action and tracks backup expiry. A later restoration must not silently reintroduce previously deleted active information.
