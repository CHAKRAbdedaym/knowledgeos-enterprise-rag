# Service catalog

It is not actual IAWEB.DEV material and does not represent its policies, employees or customers. All names, rules, durations and values belong only to this demonstration scenario. Do not use this text as professional or legal advice.

Fictional version: 1.0 · Reviewed: 2026-10-01 · Next review: 2027-01-01

## Three offers

The fictional catalog contains Discovery, Delivery Web and Knowledge Assist. Discovery lasts two weeks and produces a needs map, risk analysis and prioritized backlog. Delivery Web builds applications or integrations in two-week increments. Knowledge Assist organizes documents and provides a search experience with visible evidence. None of these offers guarantees that a language model answers every question correctly. Unsupported questions and insufficient evidence are considered during design rather than hidden during the final demonstration.

## Qualification

An opportunity begins with a 45-minute conversation involving a business owner and a technical contact. The qualification record identifies users, expected volume, dependencies, sensitive data and success criteria. A project without a business owner cannot enter implementation. Delivery checks available skills and capacity; Platform reviews operational constraints. An urgent request can lead to a smaller, explicitly bounded workshop. Urgency does not remove the need to understand who will accept the result or what information may be processed.

## Deliverables and exclusions

A proposal describes deliverables, acceptance criteria, indicative schedule, responsibilities and exclusions. Handover includes source code, operating instructions, important decisions and a training session. Historical migration, content writing and ongoing support are excluded unless explicitly included. This reconstruction contains no prices or daily rates. A duration and an assumed team size cannot be used to infer a quote. A prototype shown in a meeting must remain labeled as a prototype until it meets delivery criteria.

## Changes and support

A change request records value, effort, risk and schedule impact. The business owner chooses substitution, postponement or a contract amendment. Developers cannot independently promise additional scope. The standard stabilization period is ten working days after acceptance and covers defects in accepted scope. Recurring support needs a separate agreement defining coverage and service targets. Closure requires an operational owner, securely transferred access and known-issue documentation. A retrospective shares lessons without exposing customer confidential information.

## Applying and maintaining this guidance

Before acting, verify the scope, version and responsible role. Record an exception with its rationale, owner, expiration and next review. Report a contradiction to the document owner rather than silently combining incompatible instructions. A useful request states the expected result, observed situation and decision needed, without including secrets. Keep approved decisions in the relevant project or restricted administrative record. To revise this note, propose a reviewed change and update related references. These examples support retrieval experiments and portfolio interviews; they do not provide evidence of an actual deployment, real customer outcome or operating certification.

## Worked example

A prospect requests document search and a guaranteed answer to every question. The proposal separates retrieval from optional generation, includes unsupported-question behavior, and defines an acceptance scenario where the correct outcome is an explicit lack of evidence.
