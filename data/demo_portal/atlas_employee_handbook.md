# Atlas demonstration — employee handbook

> SYNTHETIC PORTFOLIO DEMONSTRATION. Atlas is a fictional intranet used to demonstrate KnowledgeOS. These are invented HR and IT examples, consistent with the fictional demonstration corpus. This is not an authentic IAWEB.DEV policy or deployed customer system. No real employee or confidential data is included.

Version 1.0. Fictional review date: 2026-11-02. Intended audience: synthetic Atlas portal personas.

## Remote work policy

Up to three remote-work days per week are allowed with manager approval. Shared collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 Paris time. Employees record their working location in the calendar. Remote work requires a managed device, a locked screen, and VPN access for private resources. Working from another country needs prior approval from Operations and the manager, including a separate security and contractual review. Remote work does not replace leave. Connectivity problems are reported to the manager so that an alternative working location can be agreed.

## Planned leave

Submit planned leave ten working days before the intended start. An absence longer than five working days should ideally be requested twenty working days ahead. Include dates and a handover plan, without personal medical details. The manager responds within three working days after checking coverage. Leave is confirmed only when approval is recorded. These timelines are fictional planning rules, not legal entitlements.

## IT access and equipment

An access request states the resource, duration, required role and resource-owner approval. Platform grants the minimum necessary rights and checks the result. Accounts are individual and use MFA where available. Temporary access expires. Devices are encrypted and automatically lock after five minutes. Lost equipment is reported immediately; never place passwords or API keys in support tickets. Support requests should describe the affected environment, observable impact and steps already attempted.

## Unsupported topics

This document does not define salaries, salary increases, pension contributions, national legal entitlements, medical benefits or executive decisions. The assistant must abstain when the indexed evidence does not support a requested fact. Similarity scores measure retrieval similarity and do not certify that an answer is true.
